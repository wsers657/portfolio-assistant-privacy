Hermes NotebookLM Sync

A private, single-user bridge that exports explicitly selected Markdown knowledge notes into a dedicated Google Drive file. That file can be added as a source in the owner's NotebookLM notebook.

Privacy notice · Use information

Privacy notice

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

The bridge requests only Google's drive.file permission: access to files created by, or explicitly made available to, this application. It does not request access to the entire Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or calendar.

The current export is limited to two selected shared knowledge guides. Original notes remain in the owner's existing local/OneDrive knowledge vault. Private notes, school material, raw conversation journals, and recognised credential patterns are excluded by the export checks. These checks are not an exhaustive sensitive-data detector; the owner must keep the selected guides suitable for transfer to Google.

A scheduled process on the owner's private server prepares the export and updates the dedicated Drive file when its content changes. Source paths, modification dates, and hashes accompany the notes. No additional language-model call is required for export. NotebookLM processes an imported Drive source under Google's applicable service terms and privacy practices.

OAuth credentials are stored in a private file on the owner's server, restricted to its operating-system account. They are used only to authenticate the requested transfer, not written into notes or published on this site. Temporary local authentication files are removed after setup. Content and credentials are not sold or shared with advertisers. The bridge has no public registration, analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Data copied into Drive remains there until the owner removes it. Export status and content hashes remain on the server until removed by the owner. Revoking OAuth access stops future transfers but does not delete existing Drive files or previously imported notebook content. To stop use, disable the bridge, revoke its access through Google Account connections, and remove the dedicated source/file as desired.

This is a personal tool operated by its sole account owner. It is not offered as a public hosted service and does not accept other users' data. Its operator can manage the app through their own Google Cloud project and account settings.

Use information

This tool is for its owner's personal knowledge management. Only material the owner is entitled to process should be selected. Generated notebook answers require checking against the cited sources; dated notes are not proof of current system state. Availability depends on the owner's server, OAuth access, Google Drive and NotebookLM service limits. No uptime, completeness, or factual-accuracy guarantee is provided. These information notes do not replace Google's applicable service terms.

No personal name, email address, credential, or note content is published on this page.
