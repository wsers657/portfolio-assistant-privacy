Privacy Policy — Portfolio Assistant

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Operator and purpose

Portfolio Assistant is a personal project operated by its individual developer. It helps its owner prepare and, when authorised and configured, publish professional updates about verified projects and achievements. It is not an official application of LinkedIn or any educational institution and is not currently offered as a service to other users.

For questions about this personal project, use the issue tracker linked below. Do not include tokens, private documents or personal information in a public issue.

Information processed

After the owner authorises LinkedIn access, the application can receive the authenticated member identifier, name and other basic profile fields returned by LinkedIn's profile endpoint. It stores an OAuth access token, its expiry and the member identifier needed to act on the authorised account.

The assistant also processes profile information supplied by its owner, writing preferences, selected public achievement summaries and evidence references, publication drafts, dates, publication identifiers and operational records used to track outcomes and prevent duplicate posts. The owner should not supply confidential course materials or other people's personal information for publication.

How information is used

Information is used to identify the authorised account, prepare relevant professional content, publish authorised updates, enforce configured posting limits and maintain a record of publication attempts. LinkedIn's OAuth consent determines the API access granted. The prepared integration requests `openid`, `profile` and `w_member_social`; it does not request access to private messages, contacts, email addresses or company-page administration.

Publication requires a working authorisation and an enabled publication policy. Installing the assistant does not itself publish anything. Selected text may be processed by the AI provider configured in Hermes to prepare or review content. Tokens must not be included in prompts.

Storage and recipients

Working files and operational records are stored on the operator's computer or privately administered server, as configured. Access-token files in the prepared server integration have restricted filesystem permissions. This is access control, not a claim that every working file is encrypted.

LinkedIn receives API requests and any content submitted for publication. The configured hosting and AI providers may process relevant information under their own terms and privacy policies; their processing locations depend on the providers used. The operator does not sell this project's information or provide it to advertisers. Publicly published content is visible according to LinkedIn's settings and is governed by LinkedIn's own policies.

Retention and deletion

Drafts, achievement records and publication history are retained until the operator deletes them or no longer needs them for the stated purposes. Token expiry blocks further use by the prepared connector; it does not automatically delete the stored credential file. The operator can revoke access through LinkedIn, remove the token file and delete local working records. Local deletion does not remove an existing LinkedIn post; that post must be managed separately on LinkedIn.

Choices and changes

The owner can disable posting, revoke LinkedIn access and correct or delete the data they control. General questions may be submitted through the project issue tracker. The application is currently for its developer’s own use only. This policy will be updated when the integration's purpose, providers, data use or availability changes. If the application is opened to other users, the operator must first update its privacy information and data-handling arrangements to reflect that service.
